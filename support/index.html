Aevelis Support

Aevelis is published by Sanjit Rajendiran.

Contact: Open a request on the public support request page. Include the Aevelis and iOS version, but do not include medical records, genetic files, or sensitive health information.

Common questions

Where is my data?
Questionnaire answers, check-ins, family history, and genetic compatibility summaries stay on your device in this version.

Is Apple Health required?
No. Apple Health access is optional and read-only. Manual entry is available.

How do I delete data?
Delete individual items from their feature screens, or open Settings in Aevelis and choose Delete All Local Data. Deleting the app also removes its local container.

How do I restore or cancel Premium?
Use Restore Purchases in Aevelis. Manage or cancel through iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

What happens to a genetic file?
A supported file is read locally and not retained. Aevelis stores only the reported genome build, marker count, and import date; genotype calls are discarded.

Safety

Aevelis is educational wellness software, not medical advice or an emergency service. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions. For an emergency, contact local emergency services.

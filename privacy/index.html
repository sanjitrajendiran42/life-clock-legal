Aevelis · Support

Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

Aevelis is provided by Sanjit Rajendiran and is designed so your wellness information stays on your device.

Information used by the app

You may enter age, height, weight, sleep, nutrition, movement, alcohol and smoking status, mood, stress, recovery, daily check-ins, and optional family health history. Aevelis stores this information locally. The developer does not receive it, sell it, use it for advertising, or track you across apps or websites.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Aevelis requests optional read-only access to sleep, workouts, steps, active energy, exercise time, height, and weight to prefill supported fields. It does not write Health data. Processing occurs on your device and is not used for advertising or marketing.

Family history and raw genetic data

Adults may optionally record family health history and import their own supported 23andMe raw-data text file. Parsing occurs entirely on device. The selected file and genotype calls are not retained; only genome-build label, marker count, and import time are stored. Aevelis does not diagnose disease, calculate clinical risk, recommend treatment, identify relatives or ancestry, or use this information to predict lifespan. You can delete the retained summary and family-history entries separately.

Purchases and reminders

Apple processes subscriptions through the App Store; the developer does not receive card details. Optional reminders are scheduled by iOS on your device.

Analytics and third parties

This version contains no advertising, third-party analytics, developer cloud account, cloud synchronization, data broker integration, or third-party AI processing.

Retention and deletion

Local entries remain until you delete them in Aevelis, reset local data, or delete the app. Revoking Apple Health access stops future reads. Deleting an import summary does not delete the original file from Files or its document provider.

Children and safety

Aevelis is not directed to children under 13. Genetic import is for adults 18 or older importing their own file. Aevelis is an educational wellness tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, genetic counseling, or a prediction of an individual's lifespan.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, contact the publisher through the public support request page. Do not include medical records, genetic files, or sensitive health information.
